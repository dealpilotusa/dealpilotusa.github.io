About DealPilot USA

Everyday tools. Real answers. Zero noise.

What this site is

DealPilot USA is a small, independent web project. We build free browser-based tools — calculators, converters, and helpers — that solve specific, everyday problems for people in the United States. We also curate a short list of official resources and write plain-English guides.

What this site is not

We are not a price-tracking service. We are not a financial advisor. We are not a review lab. We do not publish statistics we cannot source, we do not write fake reviews, and we do not display invented user counts, earnings, or conversion numbers.

How we handle your data

Every calculator runs locally in your browser. Values you type into the percentage, tip, loan, BMI, password, and image tools never leave your device. Saved bookmarks, recent tools, and reading positions are stored in your browser's local storage only. See our Privacy Policy.

How we make money

Today: we don't. There are no ads, no affiliate links, and no paid placements on this site. If we ever add affiliate links in the future, they will be clearly disclosed on every page where they appear, and the disclosure will appear before the link — not buried in small print. See our Affiliate Disclosure.

1. Summary

DealPilot USA runs entirely in your browser. We do not operate a server that stores your inputs. The tools on this site are client-side JavaScript that compute results on your own device.

2. What we collect

By default, nothing. If you accept analytics cookies, we may use a third-party analytics provider (Google Analytics 4) to count page views and understand which tools are used. That provider receives your IP address, browser type, and pages visited — subject to their own privacy policies. 3. What stays on your device

We use your browser's localStorage to remember:

  • Which tools you've recently opened
  • Which tools you've saved with the heart button
  • Your reading positions in guides
  • Your cookie preference
  • Your visit streak and first-visit date

This data never leaves your device. You can clear it at any time with the "Reset local data" button in the footer or your browser's own settings.

The image compressor uses a canvas element that stays in your browser's memory. The password strength checker tests your password without ever sending it anywhere. All other calculators perform simple math locally.

Affiliate Disclosure

Current status: As of the version you're reading, DealPilot USA contains no affiliate links. We earn nothing from any link on this site. This page describes our policy if that ever changes.

What affiliate links are

An affiliate link is a link to a third-party merchant that includes a tracking identifier. If you make a purchase or complete an eligible action through that link, the merchant pays the referrer a commission — at no additional cost to you.

Our policy

  • Disclosed before the link. Any page containing an affiliate link will display a plain-English disclosure at the top of that page, before the link appears.
  • Never disguised. We will never dress an affiliate link up as an advertisement, a "recommended" button styled to look like an ad unit, or an editorial endorsement we don't actually hold.
  • No forced clicks. We will never use auto-clicking, pop-unders, forced redirects, or hidden overlays on affiliate links.
  • No incentivized clicks. We will never ask, encourage, or reward anyone for clicking an affiliate link or an advertisement.
  • No fake reviews. If we recommend something, we will have used it or have a specific, stated reason. We will not invent user reviews or star ratings.

The Resources Directory

The links in our US Resources Directory point directly to official government or non-profit sites. They contain no affiliate parameters, no tracking IDs, and no sponsorship. They are there because they're genuinely useful to US residents.

If we add affiliate links in the future

This page will be updated with the specific networks we work with, and every page containing an affiliate link will display an inline disclosure. You'll always know when a link is affiliate before you click it.
