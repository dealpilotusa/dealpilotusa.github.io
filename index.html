Frequently Asked Questions

Are these calculators free to use?

Yes. Every calculator is completely free. No sign-up, no email, no payment. All tools run in your browser.

Do you store my data?

No. Every calculator runs locally in your browser using JavaScript. The numbers you type never leave your device.

What is a tip calculator used for?

A tip calculator figures out how much to tip at a restaurant and splits the total bill. Standard US tipping is 15–20% for table service.

What is a healthy BMI range?

For most adults, BMI 18.5 to 24.9 is normal weight. 25 to 29.9 is overweight. 30+ is obesity. BMI is a screening tool, not a diagnosis.

How do I calculate a monthly loan payment?

Enter your loan amount, annual interest rate, and term in years. The calculator uses the standard amortization formula.

About DealPilot USA

Everyday tools. Real answers. Zero noise.

What this site is

DealPilot USA is a small independent project. We build free browser-based tools — calculators, converters, helpers — that solve specific everyday problems for people in the United States. We also curate official resources and write plain-English guides.

What this site is not

We are not a price-tracking service. We are not a financial advisor. We do not publish statistics we cannot source, and we do not write fake reviews.

How we handle your data

Every calculator runs locally in your browser. Values you type never leave your device. Saved bookmarks are stored in your browser's local storage only.

How we make money

This site displays clearly labeled advertising. We may also include affiliate links — if we do, they will be disclosed on the page before the link.
